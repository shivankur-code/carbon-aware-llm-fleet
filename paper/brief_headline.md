**Template sentence (as in the brief):**
> Reuse cut lifecycle carbon by **0.85%** in a deterministic plan, but age-related failures and drain

**Recommended replacement (sharper and more defensible):**
> GreenSKU-style reuse cut the lifecycle carbon of an LLM-inference fleet by **5.8%** on a clean grid but
> under **0.7%** on a mixed one, because operational carbon dominates GPU hosts. Reuse **survives
> reliability only if planned for**: a reliability-aware plan kept **91%** of the saving and stayed
> non-negative even with reused parts failing ~8x more than their age predicts, while a
> failure-blind plan turned carbon-negative at ~2.1x. Cascading old GPUs to batch inference
> pays only if their batch tokens-per-joule reach ~1.0x the new GPU's — which our GPU
> measurements will test.
